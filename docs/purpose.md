# Purpose

Most AI explainers stop at pictures. This repo turns each picture into a small program you can run,
change and test, so the explanation and the evidence sit together.

Who it is for:

- **Engineers moving into AI work** who want to see what "training", "attention" or "denoising"
  means in about a hundred lines each.
- **Interviewers and hiring managers** who want proof that the author can build the parts, not just
  call an API.
- **The author**, as a reference for whiteboard rounds: every claim on the evidence board traces to
  a line of code and a test.

What it is not: a library, a benchmark, or a model you would ship.
